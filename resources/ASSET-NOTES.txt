Coaches Skin Studio resources

Models and panel templates correspond to ETS2 Coaches DLC vehicles.
Game vehicle models and textures originate from SCS Software.
The local models were converted for paintjob previews.
Existing Thai paint designs were provided in this workspace by Tyson.
These resources are for creating compatible paintjobs for ETS2 with Coaches DLC.

texconv.exe is Microsoft's DirectXTex command-line image converter.
Its license is included in tools/DirectXTex-LICENSE.txt.
